# Q3 Operations Update

Quarter: Q3 2026
Owner: Priya Raman
Status: On track

## Highlights

Revenue reached $4.2M this quarter, up 18% from Q2. The team closed 27 new
accounts, the highest count in company history. Churn fell to 1.4% monthly,
down from 2.1% at the start of the year.

Support response time averaged 3.2 hours against a 4 hour target. Customer
satisfaction held steady at 4.6 out of 5 across 812 survey responses.

## Risks

Headcount: 32
Open roles: 5

Hiring for the platform team is behind plan, with 5 roles open for more than 60
days. Two enterprise renewals worth a combined $610K are still unsigned and both
decision-makers have changed since the original contracts were negotiated.

Infrastructure costs grew 24% quarter over quarter, faster than revenue. The
largest driver was vector index storage, which now accounts for 31% of the
database bill.

## Next quarter

The team plans to ship self-serve onboarding, reduce infrastructure cost per
account by 15%, and fill at least 3 of the 5 open roles. Leadership will revisit
pricing in November once the onboarding funnel has four weeks of data.
