# Home Inspection Report

Property: 482 Birchwood Lane
Inspection Date: 2026-08-01
Inspector: Dana Whitfield, License #HI-40213

## Roof

Roof: Asphalt shingle, installed in 2016 (approximately 10 years old). Minor
granule loss was observed on the south-facing slope, consistent with normal
wear for its age. No active leaks or soft spots were found. Condition: Good.

## HVAC

HVAC: Central forced-air system, unit manufactured in 2010. The air filter
was heavily soiled at the time of inspection and should be replaced
immediately. The furnace short-cycled once during testing -- recommend
servicing by a licensed HVAC technician before the next heating season.
Condition: Fair.

## Plumbing

Plumbing: Copper supply lines throughout, installed with the original 1998
construction. A slow drip was observed under the kitchen sink; recommend
tightening or replacing the supply line fitting. Water pressure measured
normal at every fixture tested. Condition: Good.

## Electrical

Electrical: 200-amp panel, appears to have been upgraded within the last five
years. Two outlets in the garage are ungrounded and should be corrected by a
licensed electrician. No other deficiencies were noted. Condition: Fair.

## Foundation

Foundation: Poured concrete. A hairline crack was observed along the north
wall; it appears cosmetic rather than structural, with no signs of water
intrusion or differential settlement. Condition: Good.

The water heater was not accessible at the time of inspection and is not
addressed in this report.
